## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>
Kodi is the premier multimedia center that allows you to manage and broadcast all your multimedia files with ease. This is the complete free version of Kodi, with all features and updates included, available for Windows users. Formerly known as XBMC, Kodi has evolved into a powerful open-source platform that goes beyond mere playback functionality.

With Kodi, you can organize your movies, series, music, podcasts, and more, all in one convenient application. Its user-friendly interface and extensive customization options make it the ideal choice for anyone looking to enhance their home entertainment experience.
